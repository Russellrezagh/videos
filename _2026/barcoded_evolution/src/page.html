A method, one step at a time

The film walks through the standard recipe of microbial experimental evolution: start from one clone, define the environment, transfer serially with a large bottleneck, freeze, replicate, and measure fitness by competition. Each step comes with the reason it exists.

It then follows DNA barcoding (Levy et al. 2015), the bench protocol for turning barcodes into fitness values (Chochinov & Nguyen Ba 2022), the drug-resistance design of Schmidlin et al. (2024), and whole-genome sequencing. Space plays and pauses; [ and ] jump between chapters.
